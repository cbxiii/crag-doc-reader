# A COLLECTION OF FISHES FROM SAMOA.

--- SENTENCE 4 ---
By HENRY W. FOWLER AND CHARLES F. SILVESTER.

--- SENTENCE 5 ---
The specimens forming the basis of the present paper were collected at Pago Pago in the spring of 1917 by the Carnegie expedition to Samoa.

--- SENTENCE 6 ---
Efforts were made chiefly to secure small or inconspicuous forms, and, though the collection embraces only 53 species, several are rare and one species is described as new.

--- SENTENCE 7 ---
The collection consists of five lots of small fishes taken from the following localities.

--- SENTENCE 8 ---
First lot, April 5, 1917, from the cove just south of Aua village and 100 feet northwest of Dr. Mayor's "Aua line."

--- SENTENCE 9 ---
These were taken by lifting bunches of coral from the bottom and then breaking the coral.

--- SENTENCE 10 ---
The second lot has the same data, except a few specimens screened at the bottom with wire and mosquito screening.

--- SENTENCE 11 ---
The third lot, taken April 6, consists of specimens shaken from coral in the reef in front of the hospital, Pago Pago Harbor, Tutuila.

--- SENTENCE 12 ---
The fourth lot was obtained March 20, 1917, from tide-pools near Double Point, just west of the entrance to Pago Pago Harbor.

--- SENTENCE 13 ---
The fifth lot is simply labeled Pago Pago.

--- SENTENCE 14 ---
The collection is now contained in the Museum of the Academy

of Natural Sciences, Philadelphia.
