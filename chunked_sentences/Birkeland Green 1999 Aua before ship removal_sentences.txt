# **Resurvey of the Aua Transect Prior to the Ship Removal**

# **Charles Birkeland and Alison (ilreen**

On 16-IX October 1999, we measured 794 coral colonies and counted macroinvertebrates (in this Case all were echinoderms) in 57X quadrats (0.25 m ) taken at 10 stations along Mayor's 271 m Aua Line.

--- SENTENCE 6 ---
The most striking event that has recently occurred has been the abundant recruitment of the delicate branching coral *Acropora nana* and the common sea cucumber *Stichopus vhloionotus.

--- SENTENCE 7 ---
*  Jennifer Aicher mentioned that the abundant recruitment *Acropora nana* is widespread around the island of Tutuila this year.

--- SENTENCE 8 ---
Along the Aua Transect, 237 of the colonies counted were *Acropora nana.*

--- SENTENCE 9 ---
The 7X% increase in living coral cover and the nearly three-fold increase in coral abundance is largely a result of the abundant recruitment *oi Acropora nana* and *Pocillopora (lanae.

--- SENTENCE 10 ---
There was no terrestrial sediment observed.

--- SENTENCE 11 ---
The fine-grained substrata were all white calcium carbonate sand, apparently of biogenic marine origin.

--- SENTENCE 12 ---
The water was very clear.

--- SENTENCE 13 ---
The corals seemed to be in good health.

--- SENTENCE 14 ---
Crustose coralline algae, *possibly Porolit/ion onkoclcs,* was prevalent on all the hard substrata.

--- SENTENCE 15 ---
Crustose coralline algae is especially favorable for coral recruitment and is generally indicative of a healthy coral reef.

--- SENTENCE 16 ---
The sponges *{Dysidea herhacea)* and ascidians *{Lissoclinuni patella* and *Diplosoma siniilis)* with symbiotic algae are sensitive to turbid water and their prevalence along the transect indicates that the waters on this reef flat are not as polluted as they once were, possibly because of the extension of the cannery outfalls from the inner to the outer harbor in 1992.

--- SENTENCE 17 ---
The coral community is typical of a reef flat of this kind, with a broad array of types, both longlived and ephemeral.

--- SENTENCE 18 ---
Slow-growing, long lived species such as the mound-shaped *Pontes* were found all along the transect (Tables 1 and 2).

--- SENTENCE 19 ---
Individuals up to 50 cm in diameter were observed all along the transect, although our quadrats did not actually fall on any of these larger colonies.

--- SENTENCE 20 ---
The faster-growing, rapidly recruiting, branching species such as *Acropora* spp.

--- SENTENCE 21 ---
and *Pocillopora*  -spp. were also prevalent.

--- SENTENCE 22 ---
Recruitinent and healthy growth of corals was also observed around Vessel # X. One individual V colony of *Pocillopcna verrucosa* growing on the bottom of the wreckage was already 31 X 22 cm in colony diameter.

--- SENTENCE 23 ---
*Stichopus cliloroiiotus* is about 2 *Yi* times as common as last year (Table 4).

--- SENTENCE 24 ---
it is unusual to find small echinoderm recruits, but this year, *Stichopus chloronotus* were found as small as 2.5 cm in length.

--- SENTENCE 25 ---
In summary, the coral community growing on the reef flat along the Aua Transect is in the best shape it has been for years.

--- SENTENCE 26 ---
The permanent record of the coral community since 1917 provides science with an especially valuable long-term record of the response of coral-reef communities to various stresses.
