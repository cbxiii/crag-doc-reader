# EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 02 - -2000

### AN ORDER PROHIBITING COLLECTION AND EXPORT OF LIVE ROCK

## Section 1: Authority

This Executive Order is issued under the authority granted to the Governor in Article IV, Section 6, American Samoa Revised Constitution.

## Section 2:

--- SENTENCE 3 ---
Preamble

Whereas, preservation of our coral reefs should be a priority issue for the benefit of present and future generations; and

Whereas, American Samoa is actively involved in the conservation and protection of coral reefs through its participation on the President's Coral Reef Task Force; and

--- SENTENCE 4 ---
Whereas, a coral reef workshop in American Samoa concluded that our coral reefs are seriously over-fished, and "live rock" provides food and habitat for coral reef fish; and

--- SENTENCE 5 ---
Whereas, A.S.A.C. Section 26.0220(F) provides:

#### Coral Reefs are:

- (1) "Living marine resources and their habitats shall be protected from over-harvesting and degradation..." and
- (2) "Coral reefs shall be protected from sedimentation, over-fishing, runoff, and the impacts resulting directly and indirectly from other activities to the maximum extent feasible.

--- SENTENCE 6 ---
Degraded reefs shall be restored wherever feasible...".

## Section 3: Order

(a) The collection of any "live rock", defined as any coral or basalt rock with living organisms growing on it, from reefs in the Territory is prohibited.

- (b) Export of any live rock taken from reefs is prohibited.

--- SENTENCE 7 ---
- (c) Export of cultured live rock must be permitted by the Department of Marine and Wildlife Resources in accordance with administrative rules and regulations which that department shall promulgate.

--- SENTENCE 8 ---
Section 4: Effective Date

This order is effective upon signing.
