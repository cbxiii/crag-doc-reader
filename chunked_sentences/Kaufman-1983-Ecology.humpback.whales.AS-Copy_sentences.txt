## ECOLOGY OF HUMPB WHALES IN AMERICAN

Greg Kaufman, Pacific Whale Foundation, P.O. Box 1038, Kihei, Maui, HI 96753

Fcur years (1979, 1980, 1982, 1983) of vessel and aerial surveys were conducted in the waters surrounding American Samoa (14°S, 170°W), revealing the presence of a previously undescribed population of humpback whales (Megaptera nuvaeangliae).

--- SENTENCE 8 ---
Humpbacks were observed inhabiting the near-shore waters of primarily 100 fathans or less fran June 26 to Nov. 15 with peak concentrations occurring fran Sept. 15 to Oct. 1.

--- SENTENCE 9 ---
Animals were observed as far east as Rose Island (14°30'S, 170°35'W).

--- SENTENCE 10 ---
Interchange between two wintering grounds, one previously studied and one not, and American Samoa was observed:

--- SENTENCE 11 ---
Nine animals were tracked fran American Samoa (14°18'S, 170°35'W) to Vava'u Island (19°S, 174°10'W), Kingdom of Tonga, and two animals fran American Samoa (14°20'S, 170°50'W) to Western Samoa (13°40'S, 172°W).

--- SENTENCE 12 ---
The presence of calves and singing whales is a strong indicator that reproduction is occurring in this area.

--- SENTENCE 13 ---
Partial songs recorded in American Samoa were found to be similar to songs recorded in Tonga, but dissimilar to songs obtained earlier the same year in Hawaii, indicating that there is no interchange between North and South Pacific stocks.

--- SENTENCE 14 ---
The historical ecology of the humpback whale in American Samoa is discussed.

--- SENTENCE 15 ---

## DEMOGRAPHICS OF HUMPBACK WHALES OFF SOUTHWEST MAUI, HAWAII

Greg Kaufman<sup>1</sup> and Kevin Wood<sup>2</sup>.

--- SENTENCE 17 ---
<sup>1</sup>Pacific Whale Foundation, P.O. Box 1038, Kihei, Maui, HI 96753; <sup>2</sup>Naval Postgraduate School, Montery, CA 93943

Observations of humpback whales (Megaptera nuvaeangliae) off the southwest coast of Maui were made from 1980 through 1983 to determine general demographics of the animals utilizing this previously unstudied portion of the Hawaiian breeding habitat.

--- SENTENCE 18 ---
A total of 1218 hours of observation found, excluding single sightings, 1634 pods including 3215 animals of which 188 were calves.

--- SENTENCE 19 ---
Peak densities reached .056 animals/km² with a mean of .012 animals/km².

--- SENTENCE 20 ---
Mean pod sizes ranged fran 1.77 in 1983 to 1.07 in 1982.

--- SENTENCE 21 ---
Mean speeds ranged from 1.57 km/hr for mothers and calves to as much as 5.52 km/hr for pods with six animals.

--- SENTENCE 22 ---
Other detailed statistics recorded were direction, changes in direction and speed, behaviors, affiliations and disaffiliations.

--- SENTENCE 23 ---
A lack of net northward movement does not support other researcher conjectures that this area is a major entrance into the sheltered four island area; no "parade" of animals was seen.
