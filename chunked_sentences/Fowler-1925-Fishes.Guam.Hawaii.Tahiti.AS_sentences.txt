# Fishes of Guam, Hawaii, Samoa, and Tahiti

١

By HENRY W. Fowler

Among the scientific results of field work conducted in the Marianas Islands by Hans G. Hornbostel of the Bishop Museum staff, is a collection of fishes comprising 158 species.

--- SENTENCE 6 ---
This material, together with that obtained by Alvin Seale<sup>1</sup> in 1900, gives the Museum a fairly representative collection from that region.

--- SENTENCE 7 ---
The specimens recently received from Guam are accompanied by unusually complete data and their interest increased by color sketches from the brush of Mrs. Gertrude Hornbostel.

--- SENTENCE 8 ---
In addition to the species from Guam, it has been considered desirable to record for purposes of comparative study, forty-five species from Hawaii, thirty-six species from Samoa, and fourteen species from Tahiti, belonging to collections recently examined.

--- SENTENCE 9 ---
New species in the list from Hawaii have been recorded elsewhere<sup>2</sup>

--- SENTENCE 10 ---
and it is proposed to discuss the distribution and relationship of these and of other Pacific fishes in a more comprehensive work.<sup>3</sup>
