### Conclusions

Swains and Rose are different based on coral species, genera and lifeform data analyses.

- • Upolu, Savaii, Tutuila and Manua probably of the same habitat based on lifeforms.
- • Differences in coral species assemblages between Manua-Tutuila and Upolu-Savaii might indicate some limited connectivity.
- • In a smaller spatial scale, there might be also limited connectivity between Manua and Tutuila.

- •Population demographic patterns indicates island-effect.
- • Community assemblages, the groupings were: (1) Rose; (2) Swains; (3) Upolu-Savaii; (4) Manua-Tutuila
